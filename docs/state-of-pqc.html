Sector readiness

HNDL exposure is computed per sector from its own data lifetime, which is why two sectors with the same QRI can carry very different legal risk.

Method

Every target is probed twice: a raw TLS ClientHello offering X25519MLKEM768 (0x11EC) and a minimal RFC 4253 SSH exchange that reads SSH_MSG_KEXINIT without authenticating. The score is the negotiated posture, not the vendor's roadmap.

Scoring

  • Start at 100. A TLS endpoint without hybrid key exchange costs 40 points; legacy TLS costs 20; a weak cipher costs 15.
  • An SSH endpoint without hybrid KEX costs 15. Every additional classical-debt signal costs 5, capped at 20.
  • Grades: A+ ≥ 90, A ≥ 80, B ≥ 70, C ≥ 60, D ≥ 50, E ≥ 40, F below 40.

Harvest-now-decrypt-later

A sector is exposed when data lifetime + 1-year migration lead > years to CRQC. The data lifetime is the sector's confidentiality obligation, not the certificate lifetime. This is why healthcare and government remain exposed long after their TLS stacks are upgraded.

Limits

  • Only publicly reachable endpoints are observed. A missing host is reported, never guessed.
  • The benchmark measures the negotiated handshake, not configuration drift, certificate policy or internal estate coverage.
  • No packets beyond the handshake are sent, and no authentication is attempted.
